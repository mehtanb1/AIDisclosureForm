# AI Disclosure Form Generator

An interactive web tool designed to help academic authors, researchers, and students generate a detailed disclosure statement about their use of Artificial Intelligence in scholarly work.

[cite_start]This form is based on a conceptual framework that categorizes AI use into acceptable and potentially unacceptable practices across different stages of research and writing[cite: 1].

**[➡️ View the Live Demo Here](https://your-username.github.io/ai-disclosure-form/your-file-name.html)**

## 📜 Purpose

As AI tools become more integrated into academic work, journals and institutions increasingly require clear and transparent reporting on how these tools were used. This generator simplifies the process by providing a structured checklist that covers the entire research lifecycle, from idea generation to final edits.

## ✨ Features

* **Interactive Checklist**: Guides users through three key domains of scholarly work:
    * [cite_start]Conceptual Contributions [cite: 1]
    * [cite_start]Linguistic Assistance [cite: 1]
    * [cite_start]Research Assistance [cite: 1]
* **Dynamic Form**: Only shows relevant options as you fill it out, keeping the interface clean and user-friendly.
* **Live Preview**: A panel on the right side of the screen generates the disclosure statement in real-time as you make selections.
* [cite_start]**Detailed Reporting**: For each AI-assisted task, you can specify whether your use was "Generally Acceptable" or "May not be acceptable" based on the provided guidelines[cite: 1]. An optional "Details" box allows for further clarification.
* **Finalization**: The "Create Disclosure" button locks the form and generates a unique, 12-character alphanumeric reference number for the final statement, ensuring a static record of the disclosure.

## 🚀 How to Use

1.  **Open the live demo** using the link above.
2.  **Fill in the Manuscript and Author Details** at the top of the form.
3.  For each domain where you used AI, select **"Yes"**.
4.  [cite_start]**Check the boxes** for the specific tasks where AI was applied (e.g., "Idea Generation and Refinement", "Language Editing")[cite: 1].
5.  For each checked task, select the radio button that best describes your usage pattern.
6.  (Optional) Add specific, clarifying information in the **"Details"** text box that appears.
7.  Review the automatically generated disclosure statement in the **preview pane** on the right.
8.  When you are satisfied, click the **"Create Disclosure"** button to finalize the form and generate your reference number.
9.  You can now print this page (Ctrl+P or Cmd+P) or take a screenshot of the preview pane to save or submit your disclosure.

## 💻 Running Locally
